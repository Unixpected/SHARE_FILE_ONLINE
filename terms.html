What this service does

You upload a file (up to 200MB) and get a download link. The link works for 60 minutes. After that the file is deleted automatically and cannot be recovered.

  • Malware, viruses, or files meant to harm devices or steal data.
  • Content you do not have the right to share (pirated or copyrighted material).
  • Other people's private information shared without their consent.

You are responsible for what you upload and share. We may delete any file at any time and block anyone who breaks these rules.

Report abuse or request removal

To report a file or ask for removal, email and include the download link. We aim to act quickly on valid reports.

Privacy

  • We may show ads. Ad providers may use cookies under their own policies.
