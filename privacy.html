This website (the "Service") is operated by the owner of this website ("unixpected"), based in the Philippines ("we", "us"). Contact: . This policy explains what information is handled when you use the Service. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. Short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Uploaded files are deleted automatically 60 minutes after upload.

  • Public comments are visible to everyone and are stored until we remove them.
  • Third-party advertising (Adsterra) is shown on the site and may use cookies.

3. Information we handle

Files you upload. When you upload a file, it is stored on third-party cloud storage (Backblaze B2) under a random link. Anyone who has the link can download the file until it expires. The file and its link stop working 60 minutes after upload, and the file is deleted automatically shortly after that. We do not keep a list of links and we cannot recover deleted files.

File name and size. The file name and size are used to create the upload and download link. The file name is part of the stored file path and is deleted along with the file.

Comments and replies. If you post a public comment or reply, we store the comment text, the name you typed (if any; the name is optional and shown as "Anonymous" when left empty), the time it was posted, and the number of likes and stars it has received. Comments are public. Do not put personal, private, or sensitive information in a comment. We do not ask for your email address to comment.

Likes and stars. To remember which comments you have liked or starred, your browser saves that choice in its own local storage on your device. This is not sent to us as an identifier; only the like/star count on each comment is stored on our server.

Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting and security providers (for example Cloudflare and GitHub Pages) may process your IP address, browser type, and request details to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and keep it secure. We use your IP address temporarily for rate limiting (to limit how many uploads or comments one person can make per minute). We do not use it to build a profile of you, and we do not store it alongside uploads or comments.

Reports and emails. If you contact us or send an abuse or copyright report, we receive the information you include (such as the link, your message, and your email address if you give one). We use it only to handle your report or reply to you.

4. How we use information

We do not sell your personal information.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, clear them at any time, or use a content blocker. Blocking ads does not stop you from using the Service.

These providers may process data in countries other than where you live. By using the Service you understand that your data may be transferred and processed outside your country.

  • Reports and emails: kept only as long as needed to handle them and as the law requires.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share information about you except with the service providers listed above, or where the law requires it (for example, a valid legal request from authorities). Because the Service has no accounts and we do not track uploaders or keep a list of links, we can only disclose information we actually have.

9. Security

Files are transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and links are random and hard to guess. However, anyone with a link can download the file, so share links only with people you trust. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
